# TechStream-Solutions-Streamline-Pro-Unit-Economics
## Introduction
The company is called "TechStream Solutions", and the product is a Software as a Service (SaaS) platform named "Streamline Pro". Streamline Pro is a comprehensive project management and collaboration tool designed to help businesses manage projects, track progress, and collaborate efficiently

TechStream Solutions has been operating for several years and has gathered significant data on their costs and revenues. They are now looking to analyze their unit economics to understand the profitability of Streamline Pro on a per-customer basis.

## Goals
By performing these calculations, TechStream Solutions aims to:

Identify the profitability of acquiring and retaining customers.
Assess the efficiency of their marketing and sales strategies.
Make informed decisions on scaling their operations and optimizing their resource allocation.
This information will guide TechStream Solutions in refining their business strategies, ensuring sustainable growth, and maximizing profitability.

## Result
CAC = 1213.97 pretty high ( spending on marketing is majority)

ARPU = 266.99 the Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) is 266.99, meaning each customers generated $266.99 in revenue on average during the month.

COGS = 20264 costs of producing the goods is 20264 dola (production cost for AWS Hosting is quite high)

Gross margin = 75.59% gross margin is 75.59% which higher than 50% is considered as a pretty good indicator. It is scalable

LTV = 1986.27 Lifetime value of a customer is 1986.27 dola 

LTV/CAC = 1.636 A ratio greater than 1 indicates that the company is generating more revenue from customers than it costs to acquire them. This is a positive sign, suggesting profitability and sustainable growth. However, this is not ideal ratio
